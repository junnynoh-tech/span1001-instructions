🎥Video — Oral final simulation · 1.5–2 minutes

This is your oral final in practice form. You have one video to demonstrate everything from Chapters 1–5. The scenario gives you a natural frame — but the goal is range: variety of structures, vocabulary from multiple chapters, and fluid speech.

🎯 Scenario: Your family is visiting you at UNG for the weekend. You are planning everything — introduce them, walk through your schedule, plan activities, and describe where you're all going to stay or what you'll do. Aim to touch all five chapters naturally.
A · Who's visiting (Ch. 1 + 3)

Introduce yourself and the family members coming. Include names, relationships, ages (tener), and at least 2 adjectives per person. Use ser for characteristics.

B · Your weekly schedule (Ch. 2)

Explain your class and/or work schedule using -AR verbs and campus vocabulary. Use estar for location: Mis clases están en… Tell your family when you're free.

C · Weekend plans (Ch. 4 + 5)

Plan at least 3 activities using ir a + infinitivo. Include where you're going, with whom, and use stem-changing verbs: quiero, prefiero, podemos, volvemos.

D · Where you're staying / conditions (Ch. 5)

Describe where the family is staying. Use estar for conditions and emotions: El hotel está cómodo. Estamos emocionados. Use the present progressive for something you or your family are doing: Mi mamá está buscando restaurantes ahora mismo.

📋 Self-check before you record
☐ Used ser for identity/characteristics
☐ Used estar for location/conditions/emotions
☐ -AR verbs (at least 3 different ones)
☐ -ER/-IR verbs (at least 2)
☐ Stem-changing verb (prefiero / quiero / puedo)
☐ ir a + infinitivo for plans (3+)
☐ tener for ages or expressions
☐ Present progressive (está/estamos + -ando/-iendo)
🎙️ Oral exam format reminder: The oral final will ask you to speak fluently about topics across all chapters — not just one. If you can speak for 1.5 minutes on this scenario without stopping, you are ready. If you get stuck on a chapter, review that section in your Vistas notes before the exam.
