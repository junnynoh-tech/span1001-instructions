🎥Your video — 1.5 to 2 minutes

You are planning a dream weekend trip somewhere in the U.S. Record a video that connects your free-time life to this trip — and end with preferences your classmates can actually respond to.

🗺️ Choose a specific destination first. Your whole video builds around it. Be concrete — a named place, not just "the beach" or "the mountains." The more specific you are, the better the peer response will be.
A · Your free time right now

Talk about what you normally do in your free time — where you go, what you prefer, with whom. Vary your verbs: prefiero, quiero, puedo, vuelvo, salgo, hago, voy. Don't just list activities; describe them.

B · Preparing for the trip — ir a + infinitivo

What are you going to do to prepare — and once you're there? Use ir a + infinitivo for at least three plans. Include who is going, when, and what activities or places you'll visit.

C · End with your preferences — your classmates respond to this

Close by sharing a real preference tension or dilemma for the trip using first and third person. Give your classmates something concrete to weigh in on. For example:

  • Yo prefiero quedarme en un hotel barato, pero mi amiga prefiere uno mejor. ¿Qué debemos hacer?
  • Quiero ir a las montañas el sábado, pero también quiero ir a la playa. ¿Puedo hacer los dos?
  • Mis amigos quieren salir a comer cada noche, pero yo quiero cocinar. ¿Qué hacemos?

📌 Grammar to show in this video (Ch. 1–4)

Stem-changing: prefiero · quiero · puedo · vuelvo · sirvo
Irregular yo: salgo · hago · voy · digo · traigo
Plans: voy a + inf. · va a + inf. · van a + inf.
Mix persons: yo prefiero… mi amigo prefiere… nosotros vamos a…

💬After classmates post — leave a video comment
⚠️ You must submit your video before you can see classmates' videos. Once your submission is in, their videos will be visible in Bongo. If no one has posted yet, check back before the deadline.

Watch one classmate's video — especially how they ended it (their preferences in section C). Leave a video comment that engages with what they actually said.

👥 Your video comment must do all three:

  1. 1React to their destination or plans — say whether you'd want to go and why, using querer or preferir
  2. 2Respond directly to their preference or dilemma with a suggestion: Puedes… / Debes… / Tienes que…
  3. 3Add your own related preference: Yo prefiero… porque… pero también puedes…
Face visible · Spanish only · Video comment (not text)
