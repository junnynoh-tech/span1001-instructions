La familia

Introduce your family to Carmen, a friend from Mexico! Present photo slides — one per relative — and describe each person using family vocabulary, descriptive adjectives, and verbs from Chapters 1–3.

📋 Mini-quiz first 🎬 Camera + Screen in Bongo ⏱️ 1.5–2 min 👨‍👩‍👧 Min 3 relatives · 5 slides
🚦How to complete this lab
  1. 1Complete the Preparation Mini-Quiz in D2L.
  2. 2Gather photos of family members (real or fictional) and build your slides.
  3. 3Open the Lab 3: Bongo Activity in D2L via Bongo.
  4. 4Select Camera and Screen in Bongo — share your slides while recording with face visible.
💡 You may use real or fictional family members. Characters from a show or book are fine if you prefer privacy.
🏫

Want help? Do it in the Lab

Optional

🗓️ Reserve a Lab Spot
✅Requirements
  • 1.5–2 minutes of speech
  • Face visible — use Camera and Screen mode
  • Each slide must show a photo of the person you're describing
  • Speak only in Spanish
  • Complete sentences — subject + verb
  • One notecard, up to 15 words — no scripts
  • Minimum 5 slides — one per relative (plus optional title slide)
  • Each slide: photo + name + 3–5 keyword labels only
  • No full sentences on slides — your voice provides them
  • Keywords must come from Ch. 1–3 vocabulary

Describe at least 3 relatives. For each, include:

  • Relationship + name + age (Tiene… años)
  • At least 3 descriptive adjectives from Ch. 3
  • Where they live (Vive en…)
  • What they do (Trabaja / Estudia / Es…)
  • One extra detail of your choice
  • First occurrence: Incomplete — resubmit
  • Subsequent: Score of 0
🚫 No online translators or AI tools. Slides show keywords only — your speech provides the sentences.

🎬 Alternative Recording Methods
If Bongo's Camera and Screen doesn't work — use one of these
Option A — PowerPoint with Camera (recommended)

Your UNG Microsoft 365 account includes PowerPoint — no extra sign-up needed.

1Build slides in PowerPoint (available via your UNG Microsoft 365 account)
2Go to Slide Show → Record Slide Show
3Click the camera icon in the top-right — your face appears as a floating window
4Advance slides as you narrate; click Stop when done
5Export: File → Export → Create a Video → MP4
6Upload via Bongo's Upload option
Option B — Canva Present and Record
1Build slides at canva.com (free account)
2Click Present → Present and Record — face appears in corner
3Record your narration, click "End recording"
4Download as MP4 and upload via Bongo's Upload option
