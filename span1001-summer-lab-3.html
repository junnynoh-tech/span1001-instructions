✅Requirements
  • 1.5–2 minutes of spoken Spanish
  • Face visible — use Camera and Screen mode
  • Minimum 5 slides (one per relative)
  • Introduce at least 3 family members
  • For each: name, relationship, age (tener)
  • At least 2 adjectives per person — with gender agreement
  • At least one -ER or -IR verb per person
  • Speak only in Spanish
  • Use complete sentences — subject + verb
  • One notecard, up to 15 words — no scripts

A rubric is available inside Bongo when you open the assignment. Review it before recording.

🚫 No online translators or AI writing tools allowed.
🎬Video Assignment — Family Presentation for Carmen

Share your slides in Bongo using Camera and Screen and introduce your family to Carmen, a friend from Mexico. Present one relative per slide, speaking in Spanish as you advance.

🖼️ Use a real family photo, a family tree, or multiple photos — one per slide. Clipart or drawings are fine if you prefer privacy.

48 años trabajadora inteligente Atlanta

✅ Good labels

mi hermano · Carlos · 22 años · simpático · estudiante

Keywords only — you speak the sentences

❌ Not this

"Mi hermano Carlos tiene 22 años. Es muy simpático y estudia negocios."

Full sentences on slides = reading, not presenting
