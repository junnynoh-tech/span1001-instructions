🖥️ Camera + Screen
🖥️ Camera + Screen Recording
📋What to do
  • 1️⃣Complete the Preparation Mini-Quiz in D2L first — it is part of this lab grade.
  • 2️⃣Open the Lab 2: Bongo Activity link below. Select Camera + Screen mode, allow camera and microphone access, and record your video.
  • 3️⃣Review your recording before submitting. Submit when ready — you can reset and re-record using the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right before the deadline.
⚙️Technical requirements
  • 🎥Recording mode: Camera + Screen. Open the assignment in Bongo and select this mode. Allow camera and microphone access when prompted.
  • 🖥️Slides required. Your slides must be visible on screen while you present. Prepare your slides in advance — PowerPoint, Google Slides, or any full-screen presentation works.
  • 👤Face + audio required. Your face must be visible and your voice clearly audible throughout. Recordings that do not meet this requirement will not receive credit.
  • 🇪🇸Spanish only. Do not switch to English at any point during the recording.
  • ⏱️Length: Follow the time requirement listed in the assignment rubric inside Bongo.
  • ⋮Reset if needed: Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right of the Bongo page → Reset Submission to re-record before the deadline.
🛠️Help
🔧 Bongo Troubleshooting
Try these steps before contacting the Language Lab
Before you start
💻
Use Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop. iPads and phones are not fully supported.
🔓
Allow camera and microphone access when Chrome prompts you. If you blocked it, click the camera icon in the address bar to reset.
🔇
Close other apps using your mic or camera — Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, etc.
Recorder won't load
🗑️
Clear cache and cookies in Chrome → reload the page.
🕵️
Try Chrome Incognito mode — this bypasses most extension conflicts.
Upload instead of recording live
📤
If recording in Bongo keeps failing, click Upload Video in the assignment. Record locally using your phone camera or laptop's camera app, then upload the file.
Resetting your submission
⋮
Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right of the Bongo assignment page → Reset Submission. You can do this any time before the deadline.
Still not working?
📧
Email the Language Lab before the deadline — not after. Include your name, CRN, and a description of the issue.
🎬 Record a Video with PowerPoint
Use this if Camera + Screen recording fails in Bongo
Access PowerPoint Online (free via UNG)
🎓
UNG provides Microsoft 365 to all students. Go to office.com → sign in with your UNG email (yourname@northgeorgia.edu) → click PowerPoint.
💾
Or use the PowerPoint desktop app if it is installed on your computer.
Record your presentation
  1. 1Open or create your presentation. Go to the Record tab (or Slide Show → Record Slide Show).
  2. 2Click Record from Beginning. Make sure your camera and microphone are enabled in the top toolbar.
  3. 3Advance slides manually using arrow keys. Speak clearly — your face and voice must both be visible and audible.
  4. 4Click Stop when finished. Go to File → Export → Export to Video (or Create a Video) and save as an .mp4 file.
  5. 5In D2L, open the Bongo assignment → click Upload Video → select your .mp4 file.
⏱️
Keep your exported video under 500 MB. Use Standard HD (720p) quality when exporting.
