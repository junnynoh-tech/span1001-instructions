🎥 Your video — Campus Tour for Andrea
🎬Camera and Screen mode in Bongo

Your friend Andrea from Colombia is visiting your university for the first time. You are her tour guide! Create slides and present in Bongo using Camera and Screen mode.

🖥️ Camera and Screen mode captures both your face and your slides simultaneously. In Bongo, choose this option before recording — it requires Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop.
🖼️What a good slide looks like
📷 Slide Example
La biblioteca
estudiar leer tranquilo
"Aquí está la biblioteca. En la biblioteca, los estudiantes estudian y leen libros."
🔊
✅ Good slide

la cafetería
caminar · almorzar · conversar

Keywords only — you speak the sentences

❌ Not this

"En la cafetería los estudiantes caminan y almuerzan juntos todos los días."

Full sentences on slides = reading, not presenting

✅Requirements
  • 1
    4–5 campus or community places — each on its own slide with a photo
  • 2
    For each place: name it in Spanish, use estar for location, and describe what people do there with at least two -AR verbs
  • 3
    Minimum 5 slides total (title slide + places)
  • 4
    Slides contain keywords only — your spoken sentences provide the full description
  • 5
    Face visible throughout — Camera and Screen captures both your face and your slides
