🏞️Video 1 — Be Rafa's tour guide · 1.5–2 minutes

Rafa is visiting North Georgia for the first time. Make him want to come. Use named places, real details, actual prices — and Ch. 5 vocabulary throughout.

💡 Use estar for location and conditions, the present progressive for what people are doing, and ser for characteristics. El lago está tranquilo ahora mismo. Los turistas están caminando. Amicalola es impresionante.
🎤
Introduce yourself

Who are you? Why should Rafa trust your recommendations?

🏔️
At least 2 places to visit
  • Location (estar) and characteristics (ser)
  • What people are doing right now — present progressive
  • When it's open
🏨
A hotel recommendation
  • Name it and say where it is (estar)
  • Describe its condition: Está limpio, cómodo, ordenado…
  • Approximate cost per night
🍽️
Food + one activity

A restaurant recommendation and one specific activity. Be concrete.

🌐 Online students: Use UNG's website, Google Maps, or local tourism sites. Your spoken Spanish is what matters.
🌴Video 2 — Your own vacation plans

Rafa's visit inspired you. Plan your own trip — and end with a preference your classmates can respond to.

A · The trip

Where are you going? How — en avión, en auto, en autobús? Use ir a + infinitivo for at least 3 plans. How do you feel? — Estoy emocionado/a, nervioso/a, impaciente…

B · The hotel

Where are you staying? Is it expensive? In good condition? El hotel está limpio y ordenado. Es moderno. Mention the cost.

C · The weather

Va a hacer calor / frío / buen tiempo… How does that affect your plans?

D · Your preferences — classmates respond to this

Close with a real dilemma or preference tension using 1st and 3rd person:

  • Prefiero un hotel barato, pero mi compañero prefiere uno más cómodo.
  • Quiero ir de excursión, pero también quiero descansar. ¿Qué hago?
  • Mis amigos quieren acampar, pero yo prefiero el hotel. ¿Qué debo hacer?
💡 A dream trip is fine. What matters is using Ch. 5 vocabulary: estar for emotions and conditions, ser vs. estar, present progressive, vacation vocabulary.

Watch one classmate's video — especially section D. Leave a video comment that engages with their preference or dilemma.
