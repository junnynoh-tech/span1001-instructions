🎥 Lab 1 — Mi presentación
Record a 1.5–2 minute introduction

Record one continuous video in Spanish. Cover all eight of the elements below — in any order, in your own words. This is a real introduction, not a script. The content must be yours.

1 · Greeting + Name

Greet your audience and say your full name. Use a natural greeting — not just "hola."

2 · Origin

Say where you are from — city, state, or country. Use ser de.

3 · Occupation or Major

Say what you are or what you study — student, nurse, business major, etc. Use ser. If you name a field of study, link it with de: ✓ Soy estudiante de enfermería — ✗ Soy estudiante enfermería.

4 · Your Classes

Say what classes you are taking this semester — name two or three subjects. Use ser: Mis clases son [materia], [materia] y [materia].

5 · Your Schedule

Tell the time of your primera clase (first class) and your última clase (last class) of the day. If your classes are online, tell the time of two or more events this week instead — a practice, a meeting, a party. Use ser: Mi primera clase es a las… · Mi última clase es a las…

6 · Something Interesting

Share one surprising or unique fact about yourself using ser. Nationality? An unexpected hobby or title? Something classmates won't predict.

7 · Introduce a Real Person

Introduce someone real in your life — a friend, roommate, sibling, or professor: Te presento a [nombre].

8 · About That Person

Say where they are from and what they do or study: Es de… · Es estudiante de…

Optional: hold up a photo of them while you talk.

📌 Grammar focus for this video

  • ser for identity and origin: Soy de… · Soy estudiante de…
  • Don't drop the de before a field of study or class subject — Spanish always links them: ✓ estudiante de enfermería · clase de español, never ✗ estudiante enfermería · clase español
  • primera (first) / última (last) — use ser to give two times: Mi primera clase es a las ocho. · Mi última clase es a las dos. Online? Two events instead: Mi práctica es el lunes a las diez. · Mi reunión es el jueves a la una.
  • Te presento a… — a fixed expression used the same way every time, regardless of who you're introducing
  • Switch to es when describing the other person: Es de… · Es estudiante de… — not soy, which is only for yourself
Your video needs to cover — in any order, in your own words:
quién eres de dónde eres qué eres / estudias cuáles son tus clases tu primera y última clase (o dos eventos) algo interesante sobre ti presentar a alguien real de dónde es y qué es esa persona

The order is yours — start anywhere. Build sentences yourself using the Grammar Focus and Key Vocabulary above.

💡 Your video should sound like you — not like you're filling in blanks. Practise out loud many times before recording.
