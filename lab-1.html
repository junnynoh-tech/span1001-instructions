🎥 Part 1 — Preséntate
Record a 60–90 second introduction

Introduce yourself in Spanish. Cover all six of the elements below — in any order, in your own words. This is a real conversation, not a script. The content must be yours.

1 · Greeting + Name

Greet your audience and say your full name. Use a natural greeting — not just "hola."

2 · Origin

Say where you are from — city, state, or country. Use ser de.

3 · Occupation or Title

Say what you are — student, worker, athlete, etc. Use ser. Pick from the Word Bank or invent one.

4 · Number of Classes

Say how many classes you are taking this semester — in Spanish numbers (0–30).

5 · Tell the Time

Say what time right now it is, or what time one of your classes meets. Use son las… / es la una…

6 · Something Interesting

Share one surprising or unique fact about yourself using ser. Nationality? An unexpected title? Something classmates won't predict.

📌 Grammar focus for this video

  • ser for identity and origin: Soy de… · Soy estudiante de…
  • Numbers 0–30: needed for your class count — say them aloud before recording
  • Telling time: Son las dos. · Es la una y media. · Son las tres de la tarde.
  • tomo (I take): you'll learn -AR verbs fully in Chapter 2, but tomo clases is a useful expression for this video
Structure — fill in with your own content

[Greeting]. [Your name]. Soy de [origin].
Soy [occupation/title].
Tomo [number] clases este semestre.
Son las [time] / Es la una…
[Interesting fact using ser.]

This shows the structure only — the content and order are yours to decide.

💡 Use the structure above as a starting point, then make it your own. Your video should sound natural, not like you're reading brackets off a screen.
💬 Part 2 — Te presento a…
Introduce a classmate via video comment
⚠️ Submit your video first. Your classmates' videos only become visible in Bongo after you have submitted your own Part 1 video. Once you can see them, watch at least two, then choose one to respond to with a video comment. If no one has posted yet, check back before the deadline.

In your comment video, introduce the classmate you watched to a third student. Include all five:

  • 1
    Greet the classmate by name — Hola, [nombre]…
  • 2
    Introduce a third student — Te presento a [nombre].
  • 3
    Where that person is from — Es de…
  • 4
    Their occupation or major — Es estudiante de… / Es…
  • 5
    Two similarities between both — use Ustedes son… or Son de…

Hola, Carlos.

Te presento a Ana.

Es de Georgia.

Es estudiante de español.

Ustedes son estudiantes.

Son de Georgia.

¡Chau!
