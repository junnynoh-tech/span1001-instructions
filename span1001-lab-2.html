La universidad

Give your Colombian friend Andrea a campus tour! Present slides of 4–5 different places, describe what people do there, and speak over them using -AR verbs and campus vocabulary from Chapters 1 and 2.

📋 Mini-quiz first 🎬 Camera + Screen in Bongo ⏱️ 1.5–2 min 🖼️ Min 5 slides · 4–5 places
🚦How to complete this lab
  1. 1Complete the Preparation Mini-Quiz in D2L.
  2. 2Build your slide deck (minimum 5 slides, 1 per place).
  3. 3Open the Lab 2: Bongo Activity in D2L via Bongo.
  4. 4In Bongo, select Camera and Screen to share your slides while recording with your face visible.
🖥️ Camera and Screen is the primary recording method — it lets Bongo capture both your face and your slides simultaneously. No download or upload required.

✅Requirements
  • 1.5–2 minutes of speech
  • Face visible throughout — use Camera and Screen mode
  • Slides must be visible while you are speaking
  • Speak only in Spanish
  • Use complete sentences — subject + verb
  • One notecard, up to 15 words allowed — no scripts
  • Minimum 5 slides, one per place
  • Each slide: place name + image + 3–5 keyword labels only
  • No full sentences on slides — you say the sentences
  • Labels must come from Chapter 1–2 vocabulary
  • Cover at least 4–5 different campus or community places
  • Use at least 5 different -AR verbs
  • For each place, describe: what people do, when it's busy, why it's popular
  • Use estar for at least one location: La biblioteca está en…
  • First occurrence (no face / no audio): Incomplete — resubmit
  • Subsequent: Score of 0
🚫 No online translators or AI tools. Slides show keywords only from Ch. 1–2 — your speech provides the sentences.

🎬 Alternative Recording Methods
If Bongo's Camera and Screen doesn't work, use one of these options
Option A — PowerPoint with Camera (recommended)

Your UNG Microsoft 365 account includes PowerPoint — no extra sign-up needed.

1Build your slides in PowerPoint (available via UNG Microsoft 365)
2Go to Slide Show → Record Slide Show
3Click the camera icon in the top-right corner — your face appears as a floating window
4Advance slides as you narrate; click Stop when done
5Export: File → Export → Create a Video → save as MP4
6Upload the MP4 via Bongo's Upload option
Option B — Canva Present and Record
1Create slides at canva.com (free account)
2Click Present → Present and Record — face appears in corner
3Speak through each slide, then click "End recording"
4Download as MP4 and upload via Bongo's Upload option
🎨 Go to Canva
💡 Both options produce an MP4 file you upload through Bongo. Your face must be visible in the recording.
